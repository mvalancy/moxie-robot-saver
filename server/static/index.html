🎨 Moxie's look

Moxie's face is built from layers. Pick a colour and it goes down with the next settings push — Moxie re-draws its face rather than reusing the old picture.

Advanced: layer names

Our recovered documents name all fourteen layers but list the actual choices for only the two colours above — the rest of the artwork lives in a pack we have never read. If you know the exact names your robot uses, put them here, one per line. They are sent through untouched. Some layer names are known to crash Moxie's display, so add them one at a time.

🎭 Be Moxie

Speak through Moxie from anywhere. While this is on, Moxie stops thinking for herself and says only what you type — so there is never a second voice talking over you.

Everything typed here is said to your child in Moxie's voice and is recorded in the Safety card. What your child says back appears above as text only — no microphone or camera is streamed to whoever is driving.

📅 Today's plan

What Moxie lines up for today, and why each one is on the list. Activities without a time are daily fixtures and chat breaks — they run in order, not at the clock.

🧠 What Moxie remembers

A few durable facts per activity, written down at the end of a conversation. Moxie reads them back the next time you talk. Hover a line to correct it (✏️) or forget just that one (✕).

🎚️ Voice

Which voice Moxie speaks with, and which ears she listens with. These are the engines this appliance can actually use right now — pick one and press Test to hear it. The change applies to the next thing Moxie says; no restart, and it is remembered across one.

📦 Content packs

What Moxie can talk about, as a file you can share. Export what this Moxie has, or open somebody else's pack — you see exactly what it would change before anything is installed, and you can undo the last one.
