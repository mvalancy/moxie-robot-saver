# 🔌 test_cloud_transport sections

The sections of [`sim/test_cloud_transport.mjs`](../../../test_cloud_transport.mjs), in run order,
over one [`harness.mjs`](harness.mjs): the REAL `stub.js`, `bridge/`, `mode.js` and
`cloud-transport.js` loaded as source under a fake DOM/audio/mqtt/fetch on a virtual clock, plus
answer builders (`live`, `serve`, `said`, `ticket`, `voiced`, and `tickets`/`voicedChunk`/`chunked` for a reply with several voice chunks). By default `window.moxieAudio` is a
spy whose `isSpeaking()` is as narrow as the real one; `boot({ realVoice: true })` loads the REAL
`voice/` instead, over a fake Web Audio stack and speechSynthesis that record every sound that
starts and every one cut short (`autoplay: "policy"` adds the browser's activation rule).

- [`01_wrapper_turn.mjs`](01_wrapper_turn.mjs) — §1–2: a wrapper, not a replacement; one whole live turn.
- [`02_voice_order.mjs`](02_voice_order.mjs) — §3–4: slow speech starts no local voice and plays once; the double-voice hazard proven real, and the per-event expectation that removes it. §4b–4h: one ticket per sentence — chunks redeemed one at a time and routed in order behind chunk 0, a later chunk's failure ends the voice with no local stand-in, on the spy and on the real `voice/`. §4i–4k, on the real `voice/`: overlapping turns — a newer reply's voice starting (its chunk 0 routed, or a stub line) ends the older reply's pipeline, so nothing more of it is paid for and no sentence of it is heard after the newer reply.
- [`03_degraded.mjs`](03_degraded.mjs) — §5: every degraded path answers, and is spoken, even after a voiced turn.
- [`04_talk_scripted.mjs`](04_talk_scripted.mjs) — §6–6b: the Talk box; the consolation line is free.
- [`05_bot_control.mjs`](05_bot_control.mjs) — §7: one fresh Turnstile token per send, never a dead Send.
- [`06_voice_latch.mjs`](06_voice_latch.mjs) — §8–10, on the real `voice/`: one voice per reply (no line silent after a voiced one, no stand-in voice, a failed voice spoken locally once, a voice failure never degrades the page, the bus and replays keep their rule); the thinking filler; the audio unlock.
- [`07_turns_in_order.mjs`](07_turns_in_order.mjs) — §11, on the real `voice/`: one line at a time from the controls — two lines 300 ms apart through the Ask path are sent in turn, the second carrying the first reply's context, both heard whole in order, the third turn holding both exchanges; the mic's transcript takes the same queue; a waiting line is re-decided when its turn comes (a 429 before it: stub, never echoed twice); `sendUserTurn` still sends at once (§4i–4k's backstop); a turn whose pipeline never closes is settled by `TURN_MAX_MS` (§11e); the ears cannot be held open for ever — `earsOpen` with no `earsIdle` is released at 45 s, or at the bound mic.js names, and `earsIdle` clears it (§11f–11g).
- [`09_safety_first.mjs`](09_safety_first.mjs) — §13, on the real `voice/`: a safety line never waits behind an earlier reply (W4-S7) — a waiting line goes out the moment the earlier reply's words are back and its own reply is held behind that voice; the route's safety line (a reason body carrying words: an input block's redirect, the output floor's swap, a hurt child's referral on a refusal) is spoken next, after the sentence now playing, the earlier reply's later sentences never heard and never bought (the review's hurt-child scenario: 6.4 s against 9.9 s), through the Ask path and at the seam mic.js drives; an ordinary early line is heard after the earlier reply, unchanged; a held reply that would start a local voice never cuts her; the rule read over the server's whole closed reason set; a sentence already queued in `voice/` dropped (`dropQueuedTTS`), never the playing one; a hurt line typed while the earlier reply is still on its way, and three quick lines (the held middle reply never voiced); three ordinary quick lines heard in order (no hold deadlock); lines waiting behind a refusal answered for their own words; the Listen tap itself; the REAL route's own block, end to end; an earlier first sentence slower than the 2.5 s word wait (its words logged once); what the Listen tap releases (a waiting redirect, a held reply, ordinary or a served referral) waits for the microphone and is heard whole after the recording, as on origin/dev, for `TAP_HOLD_MAX_MS` at most (a microphone that never opens, and one that opens later); an early reply whose own voice is refused has its stand-in follow her last sentence, never cut it, and never speak over a newer voice; a Listen tap after her earlier reply is handed over keeps an early reply not yet heard (its first sentence in hand, an ordinary answer or a served referral, production-like and §13 timings) and says it whole after the recording, never handing it to `voice/` to wait behind her, and keeps its stand-in too (review round 2); what "quiet" means: her server voice only (a local stand-in is cut by a safety line at once), told the moment `voice/` ends it; a kept early reply whose words went out chat-first, a reply not early ended by the tap as before, and an early reply already audible ended as any (review round 3); and, on the whole page with the real `mic.js` (the ears harness's `bootPage`), the pretend line the failed ears compose waits for every reply in hand and for her voice, so what the tap kept — an answer, a served grown-up referral, a refused voice's stand-in, a redirect the ears held — is heard whole and never under the pretend clip or its stub answer (§13w, review round 3); and that pretend line yields to any line of the child's not yet answered when its turn comes — typed while the kept answer plays or during the recording, typed just before the microphone opened, or answered from stub.js with no POST while the brain rests, a refused turn's among them: said only if no line was sent or sent on since it was composed and no stub answer is owed or was said since, its stub answer only if that still holds after the clip, so no answer of the child's (a served grown-up referral, a redirect, an ordinary or a stub answer) is superseded, cut or talked over by it (§13x, review rounds 4 and 5).
