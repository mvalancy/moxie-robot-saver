Unofficial fan project

Bring your Moxie back to life.

Embodied shut down and every Moxie went quiet. This brings the experience back — as a 3D simulator you can talk to right now, a phone tool that re-homes a real robot to a server you control, and an example of the parent console. All of it runs in your browser.

🖥️

Simulator

A 3D Moxie — face, arms, liveness — that talks and gestures, driven by the same protocol a real robot speaks. No robot, no server needed.

Revive a robot

Have a real Moxie? Make the two QR codes — Wi-Fi, then your server — and hold them to its camera. Phone-friendly, nothing to install.

Docs explorer

Want the real thing, not just the demo?

The demo talks with a stub brain. For a robot that really converses — on the sim or a re-homed Moxie — stand up the backend: docker compose -f sim/docker-compose.yml up, give it a brain (Ollama, offline), and point it here. See the revival guide in the repo.
